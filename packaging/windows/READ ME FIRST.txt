Budget Buddy for Windows
========================

A private budgeting and tax-prep helper. Your money records stay on this computer, encrypted.
Budget Buddy organizes your records and estimates numbers to help you prepare. It is not tax advice.


INSTALL (about a minute)
------------------------
1. Unzip this whole folder first (right-click the zip, "Extract All...").
2. Double-click "Install Budget Buddy".
3. Open Budget Buddy from the Start menu or the desktop shortcut.

No admin rights and no Python needed. To try it without installing, open the "Budget Buddy" folder
and double-click "Budget Buddy.exe".

If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": this early version isn't signed by a publisher yet.
Click "More info", then "Run anyway". Only do this for a copy you downloaded from the project's
own Releases page.


FIRST STEPS
-----------
Budget Buddy opens on Home with a "Get started" list: add your accounts, bring in what you spent
(import a file from your bank, or type it in), and set monthly budgets.

It starts in a simple view. For every tool (import history, auto-sort rules, broker files, live
prices and more), click Settings at the top right and set View to "All tools".

Write down the recovery phrase Budget Buddy shows you, and keep it somewhere safe.


ON YOUR PHONE
-------------
Open Budget Buddy in your phone's web browser at home, with nothing to install:
1. On this computer, in Budget Buddy: Devices, then Other devices, then "Allow another device".
2. On your phone (on your home Wi-Fi), type the address it shows, then the code.
If Windows asks whether Budget Buddy may use the network, allow "Private networks" only.


UPDATE OR REMOVE
----------------
Update: close Budget Buddy (and Quit from its tray icon), unzip the new version and double-click
"Install Budget Buddy" again. Your data is kept.

Remove: Windows Settings, Apps, Budget Buddy, Uninstall (or double-click "Uninstall Budget Buddy"
here). Your data folder, %LOCALAPPDATA%\BudgetBuddy, is kept unless you delete it yourself.
