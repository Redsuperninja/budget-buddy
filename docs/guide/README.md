# Budget Buddy guide: for people using it

Budget Buddy helps you track your money on your computer and phone. Everything runs on your own devices; nothing goes to the cloud.

Start here, then pick the section that fits what you're doing.

## Getting started

Set up Budget Buddy on your Windows computer, then use the dashboard (web page) to enter your money.

1. [Download and install Budget Buddy](install.md) — The easy way on Windows: download, unzip, double-click Install.
2. [Start Budget Buddy](start-budget-buddy.md) — For everyday use: double-click to start; how to stop it.
3. [The desktop app](desktop-app.md) — Budget Buddy in its own window, with a tray icon; ready when Windows starts.
4. [Open the dashboard](open-the-dashboard.md) — What you'll see; the tabs along the top; folding boxes up; where to type the address.

**Trying it out with made-up data?** The tester guides are in [docs/testing](../testing/README.md).

## Your money on the computer

Add and track your accounts, spending, and income in the Budget Buddy dashboard.

1. [Accounts and transactions](accounts-and-transactions.md) — Add a bank account; record spending and income; starting balances.
2. [Import a bank file](import-a-bank-file.md) — Download a CSV from your bank; let Budget Buddy read it in.
   - [Import your brokerage activity](import-brokerage-activity.md) — Your broker's history file in one go: trades and dividends sorted for you.
   - [Import history](import-history.md) — See every file you imported; remove an outdated one.
   - [Your receipts](receipts.md) — See receipt photos from your phone; delete outdated ones.
3. [Budget categories and monthly summary](budget-categories.md) — Set up budget categories; see where your money goes each month.
4. [Budget by percentage of income](budget-by-percentage.md) — Budgets like "15% of income"; your planned monthly income; shares of income.
5. [Get a suggested budget](suggested-budget.md) — A 50/30/20 plan from your last three months; use it with one click.
6. [Sort transactions automatically](auto-sort-transactions.md) — Rules that put transactions in the right category for you.
7. [Transfers between your accounts](transfers.md) — Card payments and money into savings or retirement, linked for you; what's counted as saved.

## Taxes (estimates, not tax advice)

Track income, donations, and stock gains to help prepare for tax time.

1. [Paychecks](paychecks.md) — Log pay stubs; check that numbers add up; link deposits; year-to-date totals.
2. [Donations](donations.md) — Track gifts to charity; sort a payment into charity; what proof to keep for each gift.
3. [Stocks](stocks.md) — Record purchases and sales in any account; calculate gains and losses; spot wash sales.
   - [Dividends](dividends.md) — Dividends found in your bank files, their deposit sweeps, and hiding them from your transactions.
   - [Import stocks from a CSV file](import-stocks.md) — Add many purchases and sales at once from a spreadsheet or your broker's file.
4. [Your investments and retirement savings](investments.md) — What you hold in each account and what it's worth; 401(k), IRA and HSA contributions against the limits; deposit sweeps to check by hand.
5. [Tax summary and other deductions](tax-summary.md) — Medical bills, mortgage interest and more; one page per year; download or print it.
