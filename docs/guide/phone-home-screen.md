# What your phone's Home screen shows

Your Budget Buddy home screen on your phone displays your budget summary for the month, spending by category, and recent transactions. This guide explains each section.

## Your monthly snapshot

At the top, you see either **Left to spend** or **Over budget by**, depending on whether you're on track.

Below that are three money stats:
- **Take-home pay so far:** how much income you've received this month, and how much you expected
- **Spent:** money spent on Needs and Wants categories
- **Savings rate:** percentage of take-home pay that you saved this month

## Where your money is going

This section shows your three budget groups: **Needs**, **Wants**, and **Savings**. Each group displays:
- A progress bar showing how much of your monthly budget you've used
- The amounts spent and your target
- Whether you're on track (for example, "On track" or "Over by $50")

Tap any group to see its categories and how much you've spent in each.

If some spending on your computer has no category yet, you'll see a note like "2 transactions need a category
on your computer." Those aren't counted in **Spent** or **Left to spend** until you give them a category.

## Your accounts

Each account and its balance, as of your last sync. For a credit card or loan, a balance you owe shows as, for
example, **$50.00 owed** (or **$12.00 credit** if you paid more than you owed). Any other account below zero shows
as **overdrawn**, like **$30.00 overdrawn**. If your computer has a kind of account this version of the app doesn't
know yet, the list says how many need an app update to show.

## Recent activity

The last 10 transactions appear here, showing what you bought, when, and the amount. If you haven't synced any data yet, you'll see "No recent activity yet."

## Seeing sample data?

When you first open Budget Buddy, the phone shows **sample data** until you connect to your computer for the first time. You'll see a banner that starts "Sample data — connect and sync to see yours."

Once you've synced, this banner disappears.

## "Last synced"

Next to the **Sync now** button you'll see when your phone last synced with your computer (for example, "Last synced: 2 days ago"). If you've never synced, it says "Never synced."

See [Sync your phone](sync-your-phone.md) to understand how syncing works and how to sync now.

## Something went wrong?

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Sample data still showing | [Sync your phone](sync-your-phone.md) with your computer. The sample data will be replaced with your real budget. |
| Numbers look too low; "Spent" seems wrong | Some transactions have no category yet (look for the "need a category" note). On your computer, open Budget Buddy and assign a category to each transaction. Then sync your phone. |
| A credit card or loan shows "owed" | That's how much you owe on the account right now. This isn't a problem—it's just information. |
| "1 account needs an app update to show" | Update Budget Buddy on your phone. Until then, that account's balance isn't shown here (it's still on your computer). |
