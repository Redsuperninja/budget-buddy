# Take receipt photos on your phone

Photograph a receipt anywhere, and Budget Buddy keeps it safe on your phone until you're home. The next time your
phone syncs on your home Wi-Fi, the receipt goes to your computer, where you can see it under
[Your receipts](receipts.md).

## Take a photo

1. Open the **Add Receipt** tab.
2. Tap **Take photo**. The first time, your phone asks whether Budget Buddy may use the camera: say yes. It only
   uses the camera for receipt photos.
3. Fit the whole receipt on the screen, in good light, and tap **Take photo**.
4. Fill in what you know: the **Store**, the **Total** (like 23.99), a **Category** from your budget, a **Note**,
   and whether it's **Tax deductible** (and what kind: medical, donation, business or other). Everything except
   the photo is optional.
5. Tap **Save receipt**.

The receipt now shows under **Receipts waiting**, marked "Waiting to sync". Sync on your home Wi-Fi (see
[Sync your phone](sync-your-phone.md)) and it goes to your computer, then disappears from the list.

## Private on your phone

- The photo is made smaller before it's saved, and the details that cameras hide in photos (like where it was
  taken and which phone took it) are removed.
- While it waits, the photo and what you typed are scrambled with a key that never leaves your phone, so they
  aren't readable in a phone backup.
- If you have reminders on, they say how many receipts are waiting, never what's on them.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A receipt shows a message instead of "Waiting to sync" | Your computer couldn't use that photo. Tap **Retake photo**, or delete it. |
| "Your waiting receipts can't be read on this phone any more" | This can happen after restoring your phone from a backup. Tap **Clear waiting receipts**, then take the photos again. |
| "This photo can't be saved" | Take the photo again. If it keeps happening, check your phone has free space. |
| The camera doesn't open | Allow the camera for Budget Buddy in your phone's settings. |
