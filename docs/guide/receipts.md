# Your receipts

Receipts you photograph in the Budget Buddy phone app reach your computer when you sync. To see them, go to
**Money**, then **Receipts**: newest first, each with a small picture, the date, the shop, the total and whether
it's tax deductible.

- **See a photo full size:** click its picture. A photo your browser can't show (like an iPhone HEIC photo) has a
  **Download photo** link instead.
- **Remove an outdated receipt:** click **Delete** and confirm. The receipt and its photo are deleted for good. A
  transaction it was attached to stays.
