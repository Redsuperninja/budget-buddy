# Get a suggested budget

Not sure what to budget? Budget Buddy can suggest a budget from your own spending over the last three months.

## Open it

1. Open the dashboard, click **Budget** at the top, then the **Suggested budget** tab.
2. You'll see a plan for this month, built from the three months before it.

It works best with at least three months of transactions, sorted into categories. With less history it still
makes a suggestion, and says it's a rough start.

## How it decides

It follows the **50/30/20 guide**: about half of your take-home pay for needs, 30% for wants and 20% for savings.
It's a starting point, not a rule, so it uses your real numbers:

- **Needs** (rent, groceries, bills) keep what you actually spend. A budget can't make rent cheaper.
- **Savings** aim for 20% of your income, or more if you already save more.
- **Wants** (dining out, shopping) get what's left, up to 30%. Anything left after that goes to savings.
- Inside wants and savings, the money is shared out by what you usually spend in each category. If you save
  by moving money into a savings account, part of the savings amount is shown as money to keep moving there.

Income means your planned monthly income if you set one on the Categories page, otherwise your average income
over those months. Money you moved into your own savings accounts counts as saved.

If your numbers don't fit the guide, for example your needs take more than half your income, a note says so.

## Use it

Below the table, pick one:

- **Use as dollar amounts:** each category's budget becomes the suggested amount.
- **Use as percentages of income:** each budget becomes a share of your income, like 15%. If you haven't
  set a planned monthly income, your average income is saved as it, so budgets don't start at $0 each month.
  See [Budget by percentage of income](budget-by-percentage.md).

This replaces the budgets of all your needs, wants and savings categories. Income categories aren't changed.
You can still edit any budget afterwards on the Categories page.

## Something went wrong?

- **"There's no income to plan with yet."** Give your paycheck deposits an income category (like Paycheck), or
  set a planned monthly income on the Categories page.
- **"There's no history yet."** Add or import a few months of transactions first.
