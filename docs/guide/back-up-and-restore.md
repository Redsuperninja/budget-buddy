# Back up and restore your data

A backup is one file with all your Budget Buddy data and receipt photos, encrypted. Keep one somewhere
safe (a USB drive, for example) so a broken or lost computer doesn't take your records with it.

Backups are on the Budget Buddy computer only: open the dashboard there and go to **Devices → Backups**.

## Make a backup

1. Click **Download a backup**.
2. Your browser saves a file named like `budget-buddy-backup-2026-10-16.bbbackup`. It can take a minute if you
   have many receipt photos.
3. Copy the file somewhere safe, away from this computer.

The file is encrypted. Only this computer, or someone with your recovery phrase, can open it. Save your
recovery phrase too: click **Show my recovery phrase** at the bottom of the Backups page.

## Restore a backup

Restoring **replaces everything** in Budget Buddy with what's in the backup. Your current data isn't deleted:
it's moved to a separate folder, and the dashboard tells you where.

1. Under **Restore from a backup**, choose the backup file.
2. Click **Restore this backup**.
3. If the backup was made on another computer, type that computer's **recovery phrase** and click again.
4. Confirm. Budget Buddy checks the whole file first. If anything is wrong, nothing changes.
5. **Close Budget Buddy and start it again.** The restore finishes as it starts.

Changed your mind before restarting? Click **Cancel the restore** and nothing is replaced.

Phones you connected after the backup was made will need connecting again.

## Something went wrong?

**"That isn't a Budget Buddy backup file."** Pick the file that ends in `.bbbackup`.

**The recovery phrase isn't accepted.** Check each word's spelling and order. It must be the phrase from the
computer that made the backup.
