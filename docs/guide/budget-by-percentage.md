# Budget by percentage of income

Instead of a dollar amount, a category's budget can be a share of your income, like "15% for groceries".
When your income changes, those budgets change with it.

## Set a percentage budget

1. Open the dashboard and click **Categories**.
2. In a category's **Monthly budget** box, type a percentage with a % sign, like `15%` or `12.5%`.
3. Click **Save**.

To go back to a dollar amount, type an amount like `250.00` and click **Save**.

Income categories (like Paycheck) take dollar amounts only.

## Which income the percentage uses

Under **Planned monthly income**, you can type your usual take-home pay for a month (what lands in your bank
after taxes and deductions).

- **With a planned income:** 15% of $4,000 is $600, every month. This keeps your budgets steady, even early in
  the month before you're paid.
- **Without one:** percentages use what actually came in that month, so a budget starts at $0 and grows as
  paychecks arrive.

To stop using a planned income, empty the box and click **Save income**.

If your percentages add up to more than 100%, the Categories page warns you.

## See how you're doing

The **Home** page shows:

- each category's budget (with its percentage) and how much of it you've **used**, like 61%;
- **Needs, wants and savings:** what share of the month's income went to each. A common guide is about 50%
  needs, 30% wants and 20% savings. It's a starting point, not a rule.

Your phone shows the same budgets as dollar amounts after you sync.

## Something went wrong?

- **"Please enter a percentage from 0.01% to 100%."** Use a number with at most two decimals, like `12.5%`.
- **"Set the budget as an amount or as a percentage of income, not both."** Type one or the other.
- **"An income category's budget can't be a percentage of income."** Enter an amount instead.
