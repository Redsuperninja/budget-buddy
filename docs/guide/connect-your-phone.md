# Connect your phone to your computer

Connecting lets your phone get your latest budget from Budget Buddy on your computer. You only do this once.

> **Heads up:** pairing and syncing need the Budget Buddy program on your computer to be running and updated.

## Before you start
- Have your phone and your computer on the **same home Wi-Fi**.
- Make sure Budget Buddy is running on your computer.

## Steps
1. On your computer, open Budget Buddy and click **Pair a phone**. A code appears.
2. On your phone, open Budget Buddy and go to **Settings**, then tap **Connect to your computer**.
3. You can change the name for this phone. It's shown on your computer, so pick something you'd recognize.
4. Tap **Scan the code** and point the camera at the code on your computer screen. If your phone asks to use the
   camera, tap Allow.
5. Can't scan? Tap **Type the code instead**, type or paste the code's text, and tap **Connect**.

## Sync your phone
Syncing copies your latest budget from your computer to your phone, so Home and Taxes show **your** numbers
instead of the sample ones.

1. Be on your **home Wi-Fi** with Budget Buddy open on your computer.
2. On your phone, tap **Sync now** on the Home tab (or in Settings).
3. You'll see **Syncing...**, then **Synced just now**. **Last synced** shows how long ago it was.

Your phone syncs by itself once, right after you connect it. Until your first sync you'll see a banner saying
**Sample data**. If a sync doesn't work, your old budget stays on the phone and a message tells you what to do.
If you see **Pair again**, your computer no longer knows this phone: tap it and scan a new code.

## What "Connected to ..." means
You'll see **Connected to** and your computer's name. Your phone and computer now trust each other. Your
computer should also show "New phone paired". If you didn't just pair a phone, click **Remove** there.

## "This is a different computer than the one you connected before"
Your phone was already connected, and the code you scanned is from another computer. Tap **Connect to this one**
only if you really changed computers. Tap **Cancel** to keep things as they were.

## Something went wrong?
Here is what you might see, and what to do.

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Can't reach your computer. Make sure you're on home Wi-Fi and Budget Buddy is running on your computer. | Check both are on the same Wi-Fi and Budget Buddy is open, then try again. |
| Your computer only talks to phones on your home Wi-Fi. Connect to home Wi-Fi and try again. | Switch your phone to your home Wi-Fi. |
| Your computer didn't recognize this connection. If your computer's network changed, pair again. | Click **Pair a phone** again and scan the new code. |
| That's not a Budget Buddy code. On your computer, open Budget Buddy and click Pair a phone. | Scan the code from Budget Buddy's **Pair a phone** button. |
| That pairing code has expired. Click Pair a phone on your computer to get a new one. | Get a new code and scan it. |
| Too many tries. Wait a minute, then scan the code again. | Wait a minute and try again. |
| Something on your network got in the way. Try again on your home Wi-Fi. | Try again on your home Wi-Fi. |
| This isn't the computer this phone was paired with. Pair again if you've changed computers. | Get a new code from the right computer. |
| The clock on your phone or your computer is wrong. Turn on automatic date and time on both, then try again. | Turn on automatic date and time on both devices. |
| Budget Buddy needs an update on your phone or computer so they can talk to each other. | Update Budget Buddy on both. |
| Something went wrong. Try again in a minute. | Wait a minute and try again. |
| Budget Buddy needs the camera to scan the code. Allow the camera in your phone settings, or type the code instead. | Allow the camera in your phone's settings, or use **Type the code instead**. |
