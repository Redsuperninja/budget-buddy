# Accounts and transactions

Both pages are in the **Money** section: click **Money** at the top, then the **Transactions** or **Accounts** tab.

## Your accounts

Lists all your bank and savings accounts, and their current balances.

### Add an account

1. Scroll down to "Add an account"
2. Enter the account name (e.g., "Everyday checking")
3. Pick the type: Checking, Savings, Credit card, or other
4. Bank name is optional
5. Click **Add account**

### Record a starting balance

When you add a new account, record its starting balance:

1. Go to the **Transactions** tab
2. Add a transaction with type "Balance adjustment" for the opening balance
3. This doesn't count as income or spending

## Your transactions

Lists all the money coming in and going out, and lets you add more.

### Filter transactions

- **Account:** Click to see transactions from one account only
- **Month:** Click to see transactions from one month only

### Add a transaction

1. Scroll down to "Add a transaction"
2. Pick the account
3. Pick the date
4. Choose "Money out (spent)" or "Money in (received)"
5. Enter the amount in dollars (e.g., 12.50)
6. What was it? (e.g., "Gas", "Paycheck") — required
7. Pick a category (optional — leave it blank for now and come back later)
8. Type: "Regular", "Refund", or "Balance adjustment" (usually Regular)
9. Click **Add transaction**

### Change a category later

1. Find the transaction in the list
2. Click the "Category" dropdown and pick one
3. It saves automatically — you'll see "Saved"

### Mark a payment as a gift

If a payment was a gift, click **Mark as gift** in its row and pick the kind:

- **To a charity:** check the charity's name, the date you gave it, and how much of the payment was the gift,
  then click **Save**. The gift is added to your [donations](donations.md) for that tax year and the payment
  moves to the **Charitable giving** category.
- **A personal gift** (a present for someone): click **Save** and the payment moves to the **Gifts** category.
  No tax record is made.

Either way the payment still counts once as spending in your budget. [Sort a payment into charity](donations.md#sort-a-payment-into-charity) has the details.

## Something went wrong?

**Numbers don't look right**

- Check that transactions have the right category
- Go to **Home** and look for the note about "unassigned" transactions — assign them a category

**I can't find a transaction**

Use the filters at the top (Account or Month) to narrow down your search. Transactions are shown newest first.
