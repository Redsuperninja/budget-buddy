# Sync your phone with your computer

Syncing is how your phone gets your latest budget from your computer, and how receipts you photographed on the
go reach your computer. You do it on your **home Wi-Fi**, with Budget Buddy running on your computer.

> **Heads up:** the computer side of syncing is ready. The phone app's Sync button is coming in a later update,
> so for now there is nothing to tap on the phone.

## What goes to your phone
- This month's budget: how much you planned and spent in Needs, Wants and Savings.
- The last 60 days of transactions (date, short description, amount).
- Your account names and balances.
- Your paychecks' take-home pay for this month.
- Tax totals for this year: donations, stock gains and losses, and things you may be able to deduct. These
  are estimates to help you get ready, not tax advice.

It never includes account numbers, your notes, receipt photos, or employer names.

## What goes to your computer
Receipts you photographed on the phone: the photo and any details you typed. Each receipt arrives once. If your
phone loses the connection halfway and tries again, you won't get a second copy. Photos must be ordinary JPEG
pictures (the phone shrinks them first). Anything unreadable is sent back to the phone with a message, and the
rest still goes through.

## Is it private?
Yes. Everything is scrambled with a key that only your phone and your computer have, even on your own Wi-Fi.
Each message works only once, so nobody can record one and send it again. Only phones on your home network can
reach your computer for syncing.

## Remove a phone
In Budget Buddy on your computer, click **Remove** next to the phone. It stops working right away and its key is
deleted. To use it again, pair it again.

## If the phone says something is wrong
- **The clock on your phone or your computer is wrong:** turn on automatic date and time on both.
- **Sync got interrupted:** try again.
- **This phone isn't connected anymore / was removed:** pair it again.
