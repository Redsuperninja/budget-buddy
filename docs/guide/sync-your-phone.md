# Sync your phone with your computer

Syncing copies your latest budget from your computer to your phone, so the phone shows **your** numbers
instead of the sample ones. You do it on your **home Wi-Fi**, with Budget Buddy running on your computer.
First [connect your phone](connect-your-phone.md) (once).

## Sync now
1. Be on your home Wi-Fi with Budget Buddy running on your computer.
2. On your phone, tap **Sync now** on the Home screen (or in Settings).
3. You'll see **Syncing...**, then **Synced just now**. **Last synced** shows how long ago it was.

Your phone syncs by itself once, right after you connect it. If a sync doesn't work, your last budget stays on
the phone and a message tells you what to do. To see what each part of the Home screen means, read
[What your phone's Home screen shows](phone-home-screen.md).

## Reminders to sync
Your phone can remind you when it hasn't synced for a while. After your first sync it asks once whether it may
remind you: tap **Allow reminders**, then say yes when your phone asks about notifications.

In **Settings**, under **Remind me to sync**, pick **Every day**, **Every 2 days** or **Every 3 days** (your
computer's suggestion is marked and used until you pick), or **Off**. The reminder comes that many days after your
last sync, and if you still haven't synced, once more on each of the next two days. Never at night: one due between
9 PM and 9 AM waits until 9 AM. Each sync starts the count again.

The reminder only says it's time to sync, and how many receipts are waiting if any: no amounts or other budget
details appear on your lock screen. It's made by your phone itself; nothing is sent to your computer or anywhere
else.

## What goes to your phone
- This month's budget: how much you planned and spent in Needs, Wants and Savings.
- The last 60 days of transactions (date, short description, amount).
- Your account names and balances.
- Your paychecks' take-home pay for this month.
- Tax totals for this year: donations, stock gains and losses, and things you may be able to deduct. These
  are estimates to help you get ready, not tax advice.

It never includes account numbers, your notes, receipt photos, or employer names.

## Receipts
Taking receipt photos on the phone and sending them to your computer is coming in a later version.

## Is it private?
Yes. Everything is scrambled with a key that only your phone and your computer have, even on your own Wi-Fi.
Each message works only once, so nobody can record one and send it again. Only phones on your home network can
reach your computer for syncing.

The budget kept on your phone is scrambled too, with a key that never leaves the phone, so it isn't readable in a
phone backup. If you restore your phone from a backup or move to a new phone, the app shows sample data until you
sync again on your home Wi-Fi.

## Lost your phone?
Remove it on your computer right away: see [Manage connected phones](manage-connected-phones.md).

## If the phone says something is wrong
- **The clock on your phone or your computer is wrong:** turn on automatic date and time on both.
- **Sync got interrupted:** try again.
- **Pair again:** your computer no longer knows this phone. Tap **Pair again** and scan a new code.
