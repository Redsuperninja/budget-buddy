# Budget categories and your monthly summary

Budget Buddy sorts your money into categories so you can see where it goes each month.

## The four kinds of category

- **Needs**: things you have to pay for, like rent, groceries, utilities, and insurance.
- **Wants**: things you choose to spend on, like dining out, entertainment, and shopping.
- **Savings**: money you set aside, like an emergency fund or retirement.
- **Income**: money you earn, like your paycheck or interest.

There is also a built-in **Transfer** category for moving money between your own accounts. You can't add
your own Transfer categories.

## Adding, renaming, and setting a budget

You can add a category, rename it, and set a monthly budget for it (the amount you plan to spend, or for
Income, expect to receive). Names can't repeat, and upper or lower case doesn't make a name different. You
can't move a category to a different kind. If you picked the wrong kind, make a new category instead.

A budget can't be negative. Budgets apply to every month, so changing one also changes what past months
show.

## How the monthly summary is counted

For the month you pick, the summary shows income, spending, and savings.

- Only everyday transactions and refunds count, by the date on each one.
- **Spending** is Needs plus Wants. A refund lowers the spending in its category. A refund is never income.
- **Savings** is the Savings categories, counted the same way.
- Moving money between your own accounts is not income or spending.
- Fixing a balance, including the opening balance of a new account, is not income or spending either.
- Money you move into your own savings account isn't counted as saved yet.

## Why some money shows as "not sorted yet"

If a transaction has no category, Budget Buddy won't guess. It is left out of the totals and shown
separately as money in and money out that isn't sorted yet, with a count. The same goes for a refund filed
under an income category. Give those transactions a category and they move into the right place.

## Something went wrong?

- **"You already have a category with that name."** Pick a different name.
- **"The budget can't be negative."** Enter zero or more.
- **"Please enter the budget as a number, like 250.00."** Use a number format, like 250.00 for $250.
- **"A category's group can't be changed."** Make a new category in the group you want.
- **"Transfer is a built-in category."** Pick Needs, Wants, Savings, or Income.
- **"That category doesn't exist."** It may have been deleted. Refresh the list.
