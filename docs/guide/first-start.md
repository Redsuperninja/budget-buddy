# The first time you open Budget Buddy

The very first time you open Budget Buddy on your computer, it shows a **Setup** page instead of Home. It has a few
short steps. Every step is optional, and everything stays on this computer.

Setup appears only on the Budget Buddy computer, and only while you have no accounts or transactions and haven't
pressed **Done**. It never appears on a phone or on another computer you connected.

## The steps

1. **Start fresh, or bring a backup.** New here? Carry on. Moving from another computer? Click **Restore a backup**
   (it opens Devices, Backups). A restore finishes the next time you start Budget Buddy, and you will need the
   recovery phrase from the old computer. See [Back up and restore](back-up-and-restore.md).
2. **Save your recovery phrase.** Click **Show my recovery phrase** (make sure nobody can see your screen), write the
   words on paper or keep them in a password manager, then tick **I've written it down**. Budget Buddy can't check
   that you did; the tick only lets it stop reminding you. Don't save a photo or a file of the phrase on this computer.
3. **Your first account.** Add a checking, savings or card account, or import a file from your bank. See
   [Accounts and transactions](accounts-and-transactions.md) and [Import a bank file](import-a-bank-file.md).
4. **Your phone.** Open Budget Buddy in your phone's web browser (see [Connect your phone](connect-your-phone.md)).
   The phone app isn't in the app stores yet, so this is the only way for now.

## Leaving Setup

- **Finish later** takes you to Home. Setup won't pop up again until you next open Budget Buddy; the **Get started**
  list on Home keeps track of what's left.
- **Done** closes Setup for good. You can still open it again from the **Setup** tab on Home in the All tools view
  (Settings, View).
