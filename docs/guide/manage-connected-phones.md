# Manage connected phones

See which phones are connected to your computer, and remove any that you no longer use.

## See your connected phones

1. Open Budget Buddy on your computer (go to http://127.0.0.1:8765/)
2. Click the **Connect phone** button
3. Scroll down to "Connected phones"
4. You'll see each phone's name, when it was paired, and when it last synced

## Remove a phone

If you see a phone you don't recognize, or a phone you no longer use:

1. Find the phone in the list
2. Click **Remove**
3. The phone stops working right away, and its access key is deleted from your computer
4. The phone stays in the list marked as removed, so you can see what happened
5. You can pair it again later if you want to

## Pair it again later

Once a phone is removed, you can pair it again by following the steps in [Connect your phone](connect-your-phone.md).
