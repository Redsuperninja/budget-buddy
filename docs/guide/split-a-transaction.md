# Split a transaction

Some payments cover more than one thing. Splitting lets you put each part in its own category, so your budget is right.

## Why split

- **A store trip.** You spent $52.00. $40.00 was groceries and $12.00 was household items, like paper towels and soap. Split it into two parts: Groceries and Household.
- **A mortgage payment.** A $1,500.00 payment might be $1,000.00 of principal (the part that pays down what you owe), $300.00 of interest, and $200.00 of escrow (money your lender holds to pay property tax and insurance). Split it into three parts. See [Loan and mortgage payments](#loan-and-mortgage-payments) below.

The numbers here are examples. Use your own.

## Split a payment

1. Click **Money** at the top, then the **Transactions** tab.
2. Find the payment in the list. Type part of its name in **Search**, or pick its **Month**.
3. In the **Category** column, click **Split**. A box opens. At the top it says how much to split, like "Split $52.00 into parts."
4. In the first row, pick a category from the dropdown. Type the amount for that part, like `40.00`. Type a note if you want (it's optional).
5. In the second row, pick a category and type the rest, like `12.00`.
6. To add another part, click **Add a part**. A split can have 2 to 20 parts. To remove a part, click its **Remove** button. You can't remove a part when only 2 are left.
7. Watch the line that says **left to assign**. It shows how much of the payment isn't in a part yet. Keep going until it says **All assigned**.
8. Click **Save split**.

Type amounts as plain numbers. You don't need a minus sign, even for a refund. Budget Buddy handles that for you.

If the line says **too much**, one of your amounts is higher than the payment. Lower it.

**Save split** is greyed out until the line says **All assigned**.

## Edit a split

1. Find the split. Its **Category** column says **Split (3 parts)**, with the number of parts.
2. To see each part, move your mouse over that text. It lists each category and amount.
3. Click **Edit split**.
4. Change the parts, then click **Save split**.

## Undo a split

1. Find the split and click **Edit split**.
2. Click **Undo split**.

The payment goes back to one payment with no category. Pick a category for it in the **Category** dropdown.

## What you see after you split

- The **Category** column shows **Split (3 parts)** instead of one category name.
- Each part counts in its own category. Your budgets, your monthly summary and your spending pace all work this way. See [Budget categories and monthly summary](budget-categories.md).
- The total you spent doesn't change. Only the category totals change.
- If you filter the **Transactions** list by a category, a split payment shows up under each category it has a part in.
- A split payment doesn't appear under **Needs a category**, even if you haven't used all of it yet.
- Auto-sort rules never change a split payment. See [Sort transactions automatically](auto-sort-transactions.md).
- On your phone, a split payment shows **No category** for now. Your computer's totals are still right.

## Refunds

A refund can be split the same way. Click **Split** on the refund and put each part in its category. Each refund part takes money back out of its own category.

## What you can't split

Only regular payments and refunds can be split. The **Split** button doesn't show on other kinds of transactions. If you need to record one of these, use the place below instead.

| Kind of transaction | What to do instead |
| --- | --- |
| A transfer between your accounts, linked to its other side | Remove the transfer link first, then split it. Or leave it as a transfer. See [Transfers between your accounts](transfers.md). |
| A balance adjustment (made to match your bank's balance) | Leave it alone. It isn't spending. See [Accounts and transactions](accounts-and-transactions.md#match-your-banks-balance). |
| A paycheck deposit linked to a paycheck | Record the pay on the **Paychecks** page. See [Paychecks](paychecks.md). |
| A gift to a charity | Keep it as one payment. See [Donations](donations.md). |
| A stock trade | Record it on the **Stocks** page. See [Stocks](stocks.md). |
| A dividend | Dividends from your bank files are handled on their own. See [Dividends](dividends.md). |

A split payment also can't be marked as a gift or donation. To do that, first undo the split.

## Loan and mortgage payments

You can split a loan or mortgage payment. Put the interest in an interest category, so it counts as spending. Put the principal in the **Transfer** category, and the escrow in a category for home costs.

- Parts in the **Transfer** category don't count as spending or as saved money. That's why the principal doesn't count.
- The interest and the escrow count as spending, in their own categories.

Linking the principal part to the loan's matching deposit isn't on the screen yet. For how loan payments work today, see [Transfers between your accounts](transfers.md).

## Something went wrong?

**Save split is greyed out**

- The **left to assign** line hasn't reached **All assigned**. Check each amount. If it says **too much**, lower an amount.

**I don't see the Split button**

- Only regular payments and refunds can be split. See [What you can't split](#what-you-cant-split).

**A message says the transaction can't be split**

- Read the message. The table in [What you can't split](#what-you-cant-split) says what to do instead.

**The phone shows "No category"**

- This is expected for now. Split payments show "No category" on the phone. Your computer's budget totals are still right.
