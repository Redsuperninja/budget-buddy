New bank-file rows and transactions added without a category are sorted automatically, the longest matching text wins, a category you picked is never changed, and a "Sort transactions now" button sorts older transactions that have no category yet.

- Paychecks API (`/api/local/paychecks`, this computer only): enter, edit, and remove paychecks with every pay-stub line (taxes, before-tax and after-tax deductions), checked to add up to the cent; link a paycheck to its bank deposit without counting the money twice; and see year-to-date totals with a check for too much Social Security withheld. - Charitable donations: add, edit, and remove cash and goods donations (optionally linked to a receipt), and see a yearly summary that totals your gifts and tells you what proof to keep, such as a written acknowledgment for gifts of $250 or more, Form 8283 when goods add up to more than $500, and an appraisal for items worth more than $5,000. - Stocks: record purchases (symbol, date, shares, total cost with fees) and sales (shares and money received after fees) at `/api/local/stocks/...`. Sales use your oldest shares first (FIFO), including part of a purchase; each part is marked short term (held one year or less) or long term, counting from the day after you bought; possible wash sales are flagged without changing the numbers; and a yearly summary totals short- and long-term gains. Selling more shares than you owned gets a plain-English error, and every result is labeled an estimate, not tax advice.
